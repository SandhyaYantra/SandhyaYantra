From High-Heat Kitchens to Clean Code

Hello! I am Sandhya Yantra, a culinary professional channeling years of kitchen discipline into modern software development.

Working on fast-paced commercial kitchen lines taught me the true value of high endurance, split-second problem solving, and relentless attention to detail. Every dish requires precision in timing, temperature, and ingredients.

In software engineering, I apply that identical level of craftsmanship. Code architecture must be clean, structured, and modular to ensure lightning-fast performance and seamless user experiences.
